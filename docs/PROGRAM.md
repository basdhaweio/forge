# The Forge program

This is how Forge sets up each week and builds each session, written so a physical therapist can check it quickly. Every exercise can be swapped in the app. The injury filters are conservative defaults, not a clearance.

## Principles

1. **PT every day, and it counts.** PT is a daily quest worth more XP than any other, and a weekly goal. One PT exercise counts, and so does the whole routine. It builds the Armor stat and logs as exercise. On formal PT days (clinic or PT Pilates), one tap logs the appointment as PT, and it covers the home routine.
2. **Start small.** Each kind of training is a weekly goal that starts at once a week, and a single exercise of that kind counts. Nothing is scheduled. Goals go up one step at a time once they're being hit.
3. **Knees:** meniscus / cartilage.
   - Limit deep knee flexion under load: squats go to a box at a pain-free depth, with a default ceiling of about 90° of knee bend (thighs parallel) until the PT clears deeper. Hamstring curls stop around 90° too.
   - No twisting on a planted, bent knee: Krav footwork pivots on the ball of the foot.
   - No jumping or landing impact until cleared.
   - No deep kneeling or sitting on the heels.
4. **Back:** lumbar disc.
   - No loaded spinal flexion: no crunches, sit-ups, Russian twists or good mornings.
   - Hinges keep a neutral spine and start from an elevated position.
   - Rows are chest-supported.
   - Core work is anti-extension and anti-rotation: McGill Big 3, dead bugs, Pallof presses, carries.
   - Walking is encouraged, and the foam roller stays off the lower back.
5. **Priority muscles get more volume.** Priority areas (arms, abs, upper back) get an extra set wherever they appear.
6. **Numbers go up, slowly.**
   - Weighted lifts use double progression: add reps up to the top of the range, then add weight (+5 lb upper body, +10 lb lower).
   - Bodyweight moves aim for one more rep than your best.
   - Holds aim for your best plus 5 seconds.
7. **Pain rules.**
   - 0–2/10: go.
   - 3–4/10: fine only if it settles within 24 hours; don't progress until it does.
   - 5+/10 or sharp: stop and swap.
   - A knee that locks and stays locked (won't fully straighten): don't force it and don't train; see a doctor the same or next day.
   - Catching, giving way or new swelling: stop and call the PT.
   - Saddle numbness, new bladder, bowel or sexual dysfunction, or sciatica down both legs: emergency care.
   - Worsening leg weakness or foot drop: see a doctor promptly.

## The week

Nothing is scheduled (since October 28, 2026; the earlier day-by-day plan and higher targets weren't being hit). Each kind of training is a weekly goal, and any single exercise of that kind counts. Tapping a goal on Today lists the exercises that fit the day (home or gym kit, phase, injuries and that morning's check-in), with recent ones and the PT routine first. The PT, holds and stretch goals also offer their whole session.

**Weekly goals** (times a week):

| Goal | Home | Travel | What counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| PT | 1 | 1 | any PT exercise, the PT routine or a formal PT visit |
| Strength | 1 | 1 | any strength, core or carry exercise, or a strength session |
| Holds | 1 | 1 | any isometric hold |
| A walk | 1 | 1 | a logged walk |
| Yoga or stretch | 1 | 1 | any yoga, stretch or foam rolling |
| Protein day | 1 | 1 | a day at the protein target |
| Fast (16 h+) | 1 | 0 | a fast of 16 hours or more |
| Treat budget | ✓ | ✓ | treats within the weekly budget |
| Krav, Norwegian 4×4, Zone 2 (15+ min), Murph prep | 0 | 0 | off to start; turn them on in Settings → Program → Weekly goals |

**Ramping up:** a goal hit three weeks running offers to go up by one from the next Monday, with at most one offer a week. "Not yet" holds that goal's offer back three weeks. A target change, from the offer or from Settings, never re-scores earlier weeks.

**Every day:** check in, PT (one PT exercise counts; the routine is clamshells, the side-lying leg series and hip lifts by default, and it's editable), a stretch (any stretch, roll or yoga) and water. Up to October 27, 2026 the daily list also had two isometric holds, a walk, hitting protein and logging food; those days keep what they earned.

**Sessions**, in Train for when a whole one is wanted:

- Strength A (upper push, arms, abs), B (legs and glutes, knee- and back-smart) and C (back and arms, carries)
- Krav Maga: footwork, combatives (P1: straights, palm heels, hammerfists, elbows, knees, groin kicks, 360° defense), body conditioning
- Norwegian 4×4 on the bike (4 × 4 min at 85–95% of max heart rate, 3 min easy between)
- Knee-kind and restorative yoga, rolling and stretching
- Murph builder: a "mile" (bike), rounds of 5 pull-ups / 10 push-ups / 15 box squats, another "mile". Phase 1 caps it at 10 rounds. Cindy and Murph are the benchmarks.
- A long walk

## Adapting

- **Home is the default.** Every session is planned for the home kit: rack, TRX, heavy bag, bands, PT gear and the bike (edit it in Settings → Equipment → Home). Krav adds three heavy-bag rounds when there's a bag.
- **Gym day** (tap it on Today, for that day only): exercise picks use the gym's kit, and the next gym session is one tap away, alternating A and B:
  - **Gym day A**: lat pulldown, cable or machine row, chest press, shoulder press, cable curls and pushdowns, face pulls, Pallof press.
  - **Gym day B**: a goblet box squat and cable pull-through in Phase 1, then from Phase 2 a limited-depth leg press and a trap-bar deadlift from high handles. Also machine leg curl, partial-range leg extension, chest press, row and arms.
  - Both keep the same knee and back rules as home, and on a flared check-in the button offers the recovery session instead.
- **Check-in "grumpy" (knee or back):** gentler variants are picked first, for example a Spanish squat instead of a TRX squat, or a single-leg bridge instead of step-ups.
- **Check-in "flared":** anything not flagged fine for that joint is filtered out of every exercise list and session (daily holds drop to quad sets), and Today offers the recovery session. Opening Krav, Murph, the 4×4 or Zone 2 from its goal, or the gym session, opens the recovery session instead.
  - Knee-calm day: heel props (a check that the knee still straightens), quad sets, short-arc quads, upper body, easy blood flow.
  - Back-care day: McGill Big 3, a 15-minute walk, gentle mobility.
- **Low energy:** optional sets are trimmed and "short on time" hides the optional blocks. Both earn full credit.
- **Travel:**
  - The hotel-room kit (band, mini band, TRX, chair, wall, suitcase) or the hotel-gym kit takes over.
  - The 4×4 becomes a machine-free version: punch sprints, step jacks, mountain climbers, incline burpees.
  - Zone 2 becomes a long walk.
  - Weekly goals scale to the days away.
  - A 10-minute "travel-day reset" handles flights.
  - Forgot to turn it on (or off)? Open a past day and tap "Was away", or the trip's dates, to add, move or remove the trip. The weekly goals for those weeks rescale.

## Fuel

- **Protein:** 0.6 g per lb of bodyweight by default (about 100 g at 170 lb), which is enough while training is light. Set your own in Settings when lifting picks up.
- **Water:** logged by the glass or bottle. The daily target starts at half your bodyweight in ounces (kept between 64 and 120 oz) and can be changed in Settings. Sparkling water, plain tea and black coffee count. It is a daily quest, so it still counts on a fast day.
- **Treats:** counted, not weighed. A treat is a cookie, brownie, muffin or soda; small ones count ½ and a slice of cake or a big bakery cookie counts 2. The budget is **weekly** (5 by default) so a baking day balances out. Fruit, milk and plain yogurt don't count.
- **Meals:** built once from a list of ingredients, which totals the protein and calories, then logged with one tap. Protein can also be logged by portion (a palm of meat or fish ≈ 30 g).
- **Calories:** optional. The suggested target is estimated maintenance (Mifflin-St Jeor × 1.45) minus 300.
- **Fasting:** the weekly goal is one fast of 16 hours or more; the timer's target can be 16, 24 or 36 h. The timer shows these stages:
  - 0 h fed
  - 4 h early fast
  - 12 h metabolic switch
  - 18 h ketosis
  - 24 h deep fast (autophagy is flagged as uncertain in humans)
  - 36 h top of the range

  The timer can be started late: set when the last meal ended and the clock and stages catch up. A fast counts toward the week it started in.

  On a fast day (a fast covers 12 hours or more of it, counting the running fast through its target), protein isn't expected: the Protein day goal counts the other days (and the retired Hit protein and Log food quests were off on fast days). Water still counts.

  XP builds hour by hour after the first 12 hours (an ordinary night): 60 at 16 h, 150 at 24 h, 200 at 36 h, and nothing extra for going longer. Ending early is framed as a good call and still earns the hours you did; the running timer shows the XP so far. Don't fast on diabetes medication, when underweight or unwell, or with a history of disordered eating.

## Gamification

- **XP:**
  - Sessions: minutes × intensity × 1.2, plus sets, rounds and PRs
  - Daily quests and weekly goals
  - Fasts and measurements
  - Achievements and epic-quest steps
- **Levels:** level *n* needs 100 × (*n*−1)² XP.
- **Stats:** STR, END, AGI, MOB, GRIT, ARMOR and FUEL level up from the work that trains them.
- **Streak:** any logged session (PT included) makes a day active. Every 7 active days banks a freeze (up to 2), and a missed day spends a freeze instead of breaking the streak. There is a "Comeback" achievement for returning after a break.
- **Catching up:** everything is derived from the log, so a day filled in afterwards counts as if it had been logged at the time. It goes back into the streak, returns a freeze that was spent on it, and counts toward that week's goals.
